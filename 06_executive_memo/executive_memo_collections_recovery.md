# Executive Memo — Collections Performance Review

> **Confidential • Management Memorandum**

**Investment recommendation: ₹10 Cr**

**To:** Leadership Team  
**From:** Data Analytics  
**Date / Re:** September 1, 2026 — Is the reported 11% recovery improvement real, and where should the ₹10 Cr go?

---

## Bottom Line

**Do not invest against the reported 11% improvement.**

The 11% month-on-month figure is arithmetically real for the **Feb→Mar** step, but it is not a sustained trend. Cash collected was broadly flat across **Jan–Jul 2026**, with no statistically significant trend (**p = 0.89**).

We recommend using the ₹10 Cr to capture a measurable channel-efficiency opportunity, test the one promising targeting signal, and reserve capital until evidence improves.

---

## 1. What Happened?

**Recovery performance was stable, not structurally improving.**

SUCCESS-status cash collected ranged from approximately **₹17.0–18.9 Cr per month during Jan–Jul 2026**, while recovery rate remained within roughly **9.0–9.6%**.

The reported **11.11% improvement** traces specifically to the **Feb→Mar** step; performance then declined **7.4% from Mar→Apr**. The full-period trend is statistically indistinguishable from zero.

| Metric | Observed | Interpretation | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Recovered cash** | ₹131.6 Cr | SUCCESS payments only | High |
| **Recovery rate** | 9.0–9.6% monthly | Broadly stable Jan–Jul | High |
| **Reported improvement** | 11.11% | Feb→Mar step, not a trend | High |
| **Targeted-account conversion** | 6.8%→8.5% | Directional signal; p = 0.39 | Low–Moderate |

---

## 2. Why Did It Happen?

Nine potential drivers were tested:

- Portfolio mix
- DPD
- Risk segment
- Calling time
- Attempt frequency
- Agent/team
- Telephony vendor
- Campaign channel
- Targeting population size

**None showed a statistically or practically significant effect on recovery.**

### Channel finding

The most consequential finding is **channel neutrality**.

When actual WhatsApp, SMS, field-visit, and phone-call touches were measured directly against a 14-day payment window, all four channels converted at statistically indistinguishable rates of approximately **3.5–3.7%**.

An earlier apparent WhatsApp advantage was traced to a **campaign-labeling artifact**, rather than a real channel effect.

---

## 3. How Confident Are We?

### High confidence

- The 11% headline is **not a sustained recovery trend**.
- Independent metric definitions and formal tests agree.
- None of the nine tested dimensions currently explains a strong recovery effect.

### Lower confidence

- Positive agent/vendor/channel rankings.
- Identity-resolution and source-data limitations reduce precision.
- Borrower geography is not reliable enough for decision use.
- Several event tables require explicit timezone assumptions.

### One thread merits controlled testing

Account-level conversion drifted from **6.8% in February to 8.5% in July**.

This is directionally positive but **not statistically significant (p = 0.39)**, so it should be treated as a hypothesis, not a proven lift.

---

# 4. Recommendation — ₹10 Cr Allocation

| Allocation | Amount | Decision Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| **Digital channel mix-shift** | **₹3.0 Cr** | Shift a meaningful share of field/live-call volume toward WhatsApp/SMS; add robust campaign attribution. |
| **Randomized targeting experiment** | **₹4.0 Cr** | Use a representative holdout to test whether the 6.8%→8.5% conversion drift is real and scalable. |
| **Staged reserve** | **₹3.0 Cr** | Hold capital until the experiment identifies a validated lever; deploy to the highest-confidence opportunity. |

---

# 5. Expected Financial Impact

### Digital mix-shift — higher confidence, modest impact

Using illustrative unit-cost assumptions **(not present in the source data and requiring Finance validation)**, a meaningful shift from field/live-call activity to equally effective digital channels is estimated to reduce operating cost by approximately **₹1.2–1.6 Cr per year** without reducing recovery.

### Targeting experiment — lower confidence, upside case

If the observed **1.7-percentage-point conversion lift** is real and scales across roughly **5,700 targeted accounts per month**, incremental recovery could reach approximately **₹4–5 Cr per year**.

Because **p = 0.39**, the honest base case is closer to **₹0 until the experiment confirms the effect**.

### Break-even

On cost efficiency alone, approximately **two years** against the ₹3 Cr mix-shift investment.

If targeting is confirmed even partially, break-even on the combined **₹7 Cr deployed to the two active levers** could fall below 12 months.

---

# 6. What Management Should Do Next

1. **Stop using the 11% month-on-month figure as evidence of sustained recovery improvement.**
2. **Prioritize digital contact mix** where effectiveness is comparable and operating cost is expected to be lower.
3. **Run the targeting holdout** before scaling the apparent conversion improvement.
4. **Fix campaign attribution and measurement** so channel effectiveness can be evaluated from actual touches rather than labels.
5. **Keep ₹3 Cr in reserve** and release it only against validated evidence. Do not commit the full amount to agents, telephony infrastructure, or AI voice automation because the tested dimensions did not show a measurable effect.

---

## Important Assumptions

- Source systems contain no channel cost data.
- Digital substitution must be piloted without degrading borrower experience.
- Targeting upside assumes recovered-per-converted-account remains near current levels.
- Financial impact estimates requiring cost assumptions should be validated by Finance before investment approval.

---

### Decision Summary

**The evidence does not support treating the reported 11% improvement as structural.**

The recommended path is to **invest selectively, test causality, improve measurement, and preserve capital until a repeatable recovery lever is demonstrated.**
